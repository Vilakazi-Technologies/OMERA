# VILATECH funding playbook — memory jogger

**Purpose:** A short guide you can re-read before investor calls. It does not replace a data room or legal advice. It keeps your **story, proof, and process** aligned with rounds you see in the market ($30M–$40M is real for AI + vertical workflow — but only with the right **stage, metrics, and partners**).

**Product:** Omera (chat, forms, spreadsheets) on **Xavani** — South African-born AI.

---

## 1. Why VILATECH belongs in that conversation

Investors fund **large outcomes**, not “nice ideas.” Your bundle maps to what gets priced highly today:

| What they buy | Your answer |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Category** | Vertical AI for African business (not a thin ChatGPT wrapper) |
| **Wedge** | Forms + voice + 11 languages → distribution into banks, HR, gov, SMME |
| **Expansion** | Spreadsheet intelligence + business coach → daily use, retention |
| **Moat** | Xavani (your stack), POPIA-aware design, local language + context |
| **Market** | Unemployment → entrepreneurship; paperwork friction → GDP-level waste |

**One sentence:** *VILATECH is the operating system for starting and running a business in Africa — conversation, documents, and data in the languages people actually use.*

---

## 2. Be honest about round size vs stage

$30M–$40M usually means one of:

- **Series A/B** with strong ARR or usage (often $2M–$10M+ ARR in SaaS, or explosive consumer/AI growth), **or**
- **Strategic / AI infra** bet (model + platform + geography), **or**
- **Multi-tranche** deal (equity + venture debt + grants + strategic pilots).

If you are pre-revenue or pilot-only, the **first** goal is often **$2M–$5M seed** to prove pilots and inference — then **$20M+** as a **follow-on** with logos and MRR. Saying “$30M” on day one is fine as a **north star**; your **ask** in the room should match what you can defend with numbers.

---

## 3. The story arc (never skip a step)

1. **Pain** — Paperwork, language, and lack of business expertise block millions from formal economy.
2. **Insight** — Global AI ignores SA forms, languages, and compliance culture.
3. **Product** — Omera today: chat (Xavani), forms, CSV insights; demo languages on landing.
4. **Proof** — Live app, health check, 1–3 pilot letters of intent, usage screenshots, short demo video.
5. **Plan** — 18 months: pilots → paid seats → Xavani inference at scale → API/enterprise.
6. **Ask** — Specific amount, 18–24 month runway, 3–5 hires (AI, sales, customer success).

---

## 4. What to show in 12 minutes (demo order)

1. Landing → **Demo languages** → English / isiZulu / siSwati.
2. **Chat** — Omera · Xavani — one growth question ($0 → $100k USD framing).
3. **Form** — upload PDF → voice or type → export.
4. **Spreadsheet** — CSV → pick analysis → chart + next steps.
5. **Slide** — TAM (SA SMME + formal institutions), pricing (`docs/MONETIZATION.md`), team, ask.

Keep GPT/Claude **off stage** — investors see **your** brand (Xavani).

---

## 5. Metrics investors will ask for (prepare answers)

| Metric | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Weekly active users** / pilot users | Adoption |
| **Conversations / forms completed / CSV runs** | Depth of use |
| **Pilot LOIs or paid pilots** | Willingness to pay |
| **CAC / payback** (even modeled) | Business discipline |
| **Gross margin** after inference | AI unit economics |
| **Languages used in prod** | Differentiation |
| **Security** | `docs/security/*`, Clerk, ADRs |

If you do not have ARR yet, lead with **pilot pipeline + retention in pilot** and a **path to $X ARR in 12 months**.

---

## 6. Who to talk to (build a list of 30 names)

- **Global AI / SaaS:** funds that already backed vertical AI or emerging-market fintech.
- **Africa-focused VC:** Partech, TLcom, Norrsken, Naspers-related, Founders Factory Africa, etc. (refresh your target list quarterly).
- **Development finance / impact:** IFC-style, local DFI, youth employment mandates.
- **Strategic:** banks, insurers, telcos (distribution + pilot $ + credibility).
- **Angels:** ex-C-suite from FS/HR who feel the form pain.

**Rule:** Warm intro beats cold email. One pilot sponsor intro is worth fifty blind decks.

---

## 7. Deck outline (10 slides)

1. Title — VILATECH / Omera / Xavani  
2. Problem — unemployment + paperwork + English-only tools  
3. Solution — 3 pillars (adviser, forms, spreadsheets)  
4. Demo screenshot / 30s clip  
5. Why now — AI cost down, SA digital adoption, own model path  
6. Business model — Free → Pro → Business → Enterprise  
7. Traction — users, pilots, languages, uptime  
8. Roadmap — Xavani inference, Excel, teams, API  
9. Team — why you win  
10. Ask — amount, use of funds, milestones for next round  

---

## 8. Objections (short replies)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “OpenAI will do it” | We own workflow + SA languages + forms/CSV; Xavani is ours; data and POPIA path. |
| “Too early” | Here are pilots and demo; seed tranches de-risk. |
| “Africa is hard” | That’s the moat — we’re already live with 11 languages. |
| “Unit economics?” | Model on Xavani; Pro tiers; pilot pricing; show cost per 1k messages. |

## 9. 90-day rhythm (jog every Monday)

| Week block | Focus |
|------------|--------|
| **1–2** | Deck v2, demo languages, 5 recorded demos |
| **3–4** | 10 investor meetings, 2 strategic conversations |
| **5–8** | Close 1 paid pilot; fix Supabase/health “green” |
| **9–12** | Update data room; term sheet or second close |

---

## 10. Mindset (read before you walk in)

- You are not begging — you are **offering access** to a category winner in a large market.
- **Rejection is data** — ask what proof would change their mind; document it.
- **$30M is a outcome**, not a mood. Tie every meeting to **milestones** that unlock the next check.
- VILATECH **should** be in that headline set — when the product, pilots, and story match what those rounds require.
